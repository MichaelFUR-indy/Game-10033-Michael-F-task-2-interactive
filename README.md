# Game-10033-Michael-F-task-2-interactive
When launched the sun will follow you mouse cursor.
when on the lower Y it will make it darker out.
when on the higher y it will be nice and bright.
If you hold down the spacebar, the sun will switch into a moon.
This will reverse the background from the y level you are at.
When at the lower Y it will be light out.
But then at the higher Y it will be a full moon out!
